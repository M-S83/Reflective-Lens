# Reflective Lens

**See your coaching clearly.**

Reflective Lens is a reflection app for football coaches. It helps you think
clearly about your sessions and games, in your own words, and reflects them back
to you. It never grades you.

> **Mirror, not verdict.** The app organises what you noticed and gently helps you
> reflect. It does not judge you or your players.

---

## What it does

One journey: the coach. Everything you say can be a **voice note or typed**, and
it all comes back in **your own words**.

- Set up your clubs, teams and squads. One account can hold several clubs.
- Start a **training session, match, tournament or other**, with its intent: a
  focus, a purpose, and the things you are **hoping to see**.
- **Pick your squad** for a match (starters and game changers) or simply take
  **attendance** for training.
- **Record the match**: the score, home or away, player of the match, and who
  scored, assisted, was booked, or kept a clean sheet.
- **Capture notes** as they happen, by voice or text, before or during a session,
  or as a passing thought.
- **Reflect** in your own words. The app asks for a little more only where a note
  is thin, using open questions grounded in England Football coaching practice and
  rephrased in your voice. It always asks what you would change if you ran it
  again, and every question can be skipped.
- Get a **report** that restates only what you and your notes actually said, and
  describes what you hoped to see rather than scoring it. Export it as a PDF.
- Look back across a **week, a month or a season**, per team and per kind of
  session, because a goalkeeping session is not the team's training picture.

Players appear as members of a squad. They do not have accounts and are never
contacted. Only first names are used in anything the app writes back to you, and
a surname spoken into a voice note is removed before the text is stored.

### It learns from you

The more you use it, the more it sounds like you and the sharper its prompts. It
learns your language, including the words you use with your own players, spots
the patterns in your notes over time, and quietly brings them back into your next
reflection. All grounded in England Football coaching practice, never taught back
at you.

## Principles

- **Mirror, not verdict.** It reflects and organises. It never grades.
- **Your words, your voice.** Everything can be spoken or typed, and replies come
  back in your language.
- **Private by default.** You see only what you created. Nothing is shared inside
  the app: if you want someone to read a report, you export it and send it
  yourself.
- **Signing in costs you nothing.** One confirmation email when you create your
  account, and after that you sign in with a password and we do not email you
  again.
- **Grounded.** The reflective prompts draw on England Football coaching practice.
