# Finding five, in a coaching group

For posting where coaches already are, rather than emailing people you know.
The direct invite for someone who has already said yes is in `tester-invite.md`.

**Grant these five Complimentary, not Beta.** Free for life is `coach_comp`:
active, no end date, and the Account screen tells them "Complimentary access,
with no end date. Nothing to pay and nothing to renew." Beta counts down and
would eventually take away the thing you promised.

**What it costs you.** Haiku for the high-volume work, Sonnet only for the
reports, Whisper at $0.006 a minute. An active coach runs at roughly £1 a
month, so five of them free for life is about £60 a year, forever. Worth
knowing it is a small number rather than an open cheque, because the honest
feedback is worth more than that in an afternoon.

## The longer post

For a Facebook group, where a bit more context is normal and people scroll.

> **Looking for five coaches to break something we have built.**
>
> Most coaches reflect on the drive home and it is gone by the time they are in.
> We got fed up with that, so we built something for it.
>
> It is called Reflective Lens. After a session you talk to it for a minute, or
> type if you would rather. It tidies what you said into your own words, keeps
> track of what keeps coming up across your sessions, and asks you a couple of
> questions worth thinking about. Reports for a session, a week, a month or a
> season, and you can export any of them.
>
> **The important bit: it never marks you.** No scores, no grades, no telling
> you what you should have done. It reflects back what you actually said and
> nothing else. If it ever reads like it is assessing you, that is a fault and
> we want to hear about it.
>
> It is in beta, which means it is good enough to be useful and there will be
> rough edges. That is why we want five of you rather than fifty.
>
> **What you get:** free for life. Not a trial, not free for a year. The five
> people who help us build this do not pay for it, ever.
>
> **What we want back:** use it after real sessions, once or twice a week, and
> tell us when it gets something wrong. A one line grumble is worth more to us
> than a polite paragraph. There is a feedback box in the app that comes
> straight to us.
>
> Nothing you write is shared with anyone. Players appear as names on your own
> squad list, they have no accounts and are never contacted, and you can delete
> everything from inside the app.
>
> Comment below or send us a message and we will send you the link.
>
> reflectivelens.co.uk

## Two things not to say

**Do not say it is AI powered.** It is, and no coach has ever chosen anything
because of that. It also invites the reaction you least want, which is "so a
computer is judging me". Lead with what it does for them.

**Do not promise it will make them a better coach.** It will not, and the claim
is the exact thing the product refuses to make. It helps them see what they
already think. That is a smaller promise and a true one, and it is the reason
somebody will trust it with a bad session.
